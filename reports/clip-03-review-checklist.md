## Evaluation Protocol

Clip 03 brings the viewer from the executive model presentation of Clip 02 directly into an intimate, tactile appreciation of the bottle's material craftsmanship.

Before Clip 03 can be accepted for scroll frame slicing or used as the visual springboard for Clip 04 (Ensemble Reveal), it must receive a **PASS** on each of the 7 quality criteria below.
