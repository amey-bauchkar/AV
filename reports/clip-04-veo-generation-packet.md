## 3. Creative Direction & Scene Choreography

### Objective
Expand the visual narrative from the tight, tactile macro examination of the single hero bottle into a sophisticated executive tabletop arrangement. The viewer discovers that the hero bottle belongs to a cohesive corporate gifting suite.

### Scene Progression & Camera Path
1. **Starting Beat (0.0s – 1.0s):**
   - Begins at the macro cap and neck composition established in Frame 235 of Clip 03.
   - Respects the brushed steel luster and matte sage-green surface.
2. **Camera Pull-Back & Tilt-Down (1.0s – 3.5s):**
   - The camera executes a smooth motorized crane/dolly move: drawing backward while gently rising and tilting downward from eye-level macro to a 35°–45° high-angle tabletop perspective.
   - The field of view expands outward, revealing a honed light travertine stone display tray or warm ivory plaster surface (`#F4F5EB`).
   - The full height of the 500ml sage-green thermos bottle is revealed standing upright at the right flank.
3. **Product Ensemble Staging (2.0s – 4.0s):**
   - As the camera pulls back, the supporting gifts are revealed resting naturally on the tabletop:
     - The dark forest-green executive notebook (`PIX-NB-102`) serves as the flat, central visual anchor.
     - The dark green and chrome metal pen (`PIX-PN-1003`) rests neatly parallel along the right edge of the notebook.
     - The brushed-metal and green card holder (`PIX-CH-1001`) sits slightly in front, angled toward the viewer as a tactile metallic accent.
   - All objects remain stable on the surface, grounded by soft, natural contact shadows. There is no unnatural sliding, levitating, or spinning.
4. **Terminal Hold (4.0s – 5.0s):**
   - The camera motion decelerates into a completely stationary, locked-off commercial hold.
   - The final composition is impeccably balanced: clean negative space on the left third for the website HTML headline ("CURATED FOR TEAMS"), with the cohesive gift suite arranged harmoniously across the center and right.

### Transition Strategy (Continuous Pull-Back vs. Clean Match Cut)
- **Primary Option (Continuous Pull-Back):** If the Veo model smoothly executes the pull-back from `clip-04-reference-start.png`, this provides uninterrupted spatial flow.
- **Accepted Alternative (Clean Match Cut):** If a 100mm-to-50mm optical transition causes distortion or object popping, a clean editorial cut from the cap macro directly to a wide establishing tabletop shot (with the camera performing a slow, gentle dolly push or drift) is fully approved and aligns with high-end luxury editorial cinematography.
